About JORA

Our Business and Expertise

JORA Development Services Ltd is a Zambian company providing a range of professional services in transportation, customs clearance, real estate, facilities support and private security.

1. Transport and Customs Services: We support freight forwarding, cargo handling, transport coordination, customs documentation, customs clearance, duty and tax processing, transport documentation and logistics operations across road, sea and air transport networks.

2. Real Estate Services: Our activities include buying, selling, renting and managing properties and land, including residential houses, commercial buildings, furnished and unfurnished apartments, and property development projects.

3. Combined Facilities Support: We provide facilities support services, including interior cleaning, building maintenance, waste disposal, gardening, mail handling, reception services and laundry.

4. Private Security Services: Our business activities include security guarding, patrol services, bodyguard services, private protection and security-related support. All regulated services are subject to applicable licensing requirements.

Our goal is to provide reliable services, professional support and practical solutions that meet the needs of businesses, property owners and other clients in Zambia.
